Custom Printed Discount Vouchers & Event Tickets in Doha, Qatar

By Mars Sign & Craft · 18 July 2026 · 2 min read

On this page

  2. Security features worth considering
  3. Formats we produce
  4. Practical details people forget

Tangible incentives remain one of the most effective tools for driving immediate sales, customer footfall and event attendance. Custom printed vouchers, coupons and entrance tickets give promotional campaigns a professional, trustworthy feel that a screenshot on a phone simply does not carry.

Why physical vouchers still work

A printed voucher occupies physical space — in a wallet, on a fridge, on a desk. It creates a repeated reminder that a digital code buried in an inbox does not. For retail and food businesses in Doha competing for attention in busy malls and high streets, that persistence is the whole point.

Physical tickets also solve a practical problem at events: they work when the venue Wi-Fi does not, when a guest's phone battery dies, and when the person on the door needs to process a queue quickly.

Security features worth considering

If a voucher carries real monetary value, it needs to be difficult to copy. Options range from simple to serious: sequential numbering so every piece is individually traceable, perforated tear-off stubs for retaining a control copy, microtext that blurs on a photocopier, holographic foil patches, and UV-reactive ink that only shows under a blacklight.

For most retail promotions, sequential numbering plus a perforated stub is sufficient. Reserve foil and UV features for high-value vouchers or ticketed events where counterfeiting is a genuine risk.

Formats we produce

Standard voucher formats include credit-card size for wallets, DL for direct mail inserts, and A6 for higher-impact retail handouts. Event tickets are commonly produced as perforated books with numbered stubs, or as individual cards with a matching wristband.

Booklets bound with a stub retain a control copy for reconciliation, which matters if you need to audit redemptions afterwards. Loose tickets are cheaper but harder to track.

Practical details people forget

Print the expiry date and terms. A voucher without a clear expiry becomes an open-ended liability, and disputes over unclear terms cost more goodwill than the promotion generates.

Leave space for a stamp or signature if redemption needs manual verification. Designers routinely fill every millimetre and leave the counter staff nowhere to mark the voucher as used.

Bilingual terms. Arabic and English on the reverse ensures every customer understands the conditions, which reduces friction at the point of redemption.

We print custom vouchers, coupons, numbered event tickets and perforated booklets in Doha, with numbering, perforation and security finishing handled in-house.

Frequently asked questions

Can you print sequentially numbered tickets?

Yes. Sequential numbering is standard on our voucher and ticket work, and can be applied to the main piece, the tear-off stub, or both for reconciliation.

What security features can be added to vouchers?

Sequential numbering, perforated control stubs, microtext, holographic foil and UV-reactive ink. For most retail promotions numbering plus a perforated stub is enough; foil and UV suit high-value vouchers.

Do you produce perforated ticket books?

Yes, we produce bound ticket books with perforated numbered stubs, which let you retain a control copy for auditing redemptions after the event.
